# Ceren Orhan, PhD

**Postdoctoral Researcher | Molecular Biology | Computational Genomics | Bioinformatics**

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at the intersection of **molecular biology, genomics, bioinformatics, and precision medicine**.

My research focuses on integrating experimental biology with computational approaches to study **rare diseases, cancer, population-specific genomic variation, transcriptomics, and long-read sequencing**.

I am particularly interested in developing reproducible workflows that connect:

**genomic variation → molecular phenotype → clinical phenotype → precision medicine**

## Current Research

### Long-Read & Rare Disease Genomics

Developing and validating targeted **Oxford Nanopore sequencing** workflows for inherited metabolic and rare diseases, including alignment, coverage assessment, variant calling, filtering, annotation, and validation.

### Cancer Genomics & Precision Oncology

Integrating clinical, molecular, genomic, and transcriptomic data to investigate cancer biology and develop computational approaches for precision medicine.

### Population Genomics

Working with whole-exome and sequencing datasets to investigate population structure, genomic variation, and the impact of underrepresentation in global reference databases.

### Transcriptomics & Multi-Omics

Analyzing RNA-seq and integrating transcriptomic, proteomic, genomic, and clinical datasets to investigate disease-associated molecular mechanisms.

## Featured Project

### Breast Cancer Fuzzy-ML Reproducibility Framework

[`bc-fuzzy-ml-repro`](https://github.com/cerenorhan/bc-fuzzy-ml-repro)

A reproducible computational framework for machine-learning and fuzzy-logic analysis of breast cancer clinical data.

The workflow includes:

* clinical data preprocessing
* Logistic Regression
* Random Forest
* Support Vector Machine
* XGBoost
* cross-validation
* fuzzy classification
* SHAP-based model interpretation
* statistical analysis
* publication-oriented figures and tables
